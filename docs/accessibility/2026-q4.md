## Built-in behaviour to verify by hand

- A polite live region announces "Results ready. <Provider> is the lowest-cost option at
  $X per month." when a comparison completes.
- The verdict price counts up once; assistive technology reads only the final figure, and
  the animation is skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Every chart has a "View as table" toggle rendering a captioned `<table>`; bars, segments,
  heatmap cells and variance rows are keyboard-focusable with a full spoken label.
- Trend charts move a crosshair with ←/→ and clear it with Esc.
- Forced colours (Windows high contrast): chart marks switch to system colours with borders,
  and stacked segments alternate a hatch.
